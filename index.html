SHEET 01 / 07 — ABOUT

I like taking a problem apart until I understand why-and-how it works.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at UET Lahore, currently in my third year. Most of the work you’ll find here started the same way: I pick a problem or system that interests me and keep digging into it until the theory, calculations, and results start making sense.

From analyzing a coal-fired power cycle and studying fluid flow to designing a hydraulic turbine and a refrigerant-actuated window, I enjoy taking ideas from the fundamentals and turning them into something I can model, test, and learn from.

Outside coursework, I co-direct a 50-member ASHRAE student chapter, which means a lot of my time also goes into organizing workshops, working with people, and competitions, managing teams, and actually getting things done when a spreadsheet isn't enough.
